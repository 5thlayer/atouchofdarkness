# Changelog

Written for Consumers: what a mod building against A Touch of Darkness can use, or will see change. A published version never changes: a fix is the next patch (`docs/agents/releases.md`).

## Unreleased

- The Pillar has its own creative tab, "A Touch of Darkness", and no longer nests `libworks-runtime` or fills its Works tab.
- The Dark: a survival player in a dimension type tagged `atouchofdarkness:dark` (by default the Overworld, the Nether and the End) whose eyes are below light 5, as the open sky is at night, loses a 10 s Grace Period, which refills after 2 s of unbroken light and on respawn. When it runs out, the Babau, never seen, lands a Strike, then every 10 s in the Dark: the damage type `atouchofdarkness:strike`, 10 on Normal before armour, scaled to 0 / 5 / 15 on Peaceful / Easy / Hard by the `DamageScaling` `atouchofdarkness:strike` and the `DeathMessageType` `atouchofdarkness:strike`, which picks one of four death messages naming the Babau. Creative and Spectator players and non-players are never struck, and Night Vision isn't light.
- The sky lights the Dark: a player whose eyes see the sky is Lit at night under a full moon unless it's raining, and always under the sky of a dimension type tagged `atouchofdarkness:sky_always_lit` (by default the End). Under a roof, or in the Nether, only the light at their eyes counts.
- The Dark's cues: a player hears entering the Dark with a full Grace Period, a warning 2 s before each Strike, and each Strike, the sound events `atouchofdarkness:dark.enter`, `dark.warning` and `dark.strike`, each with a subtitle and heard by that player alone. A vignette darkens the screen's edge as the Grace Period drains and clears in light, following the payload `atouchofdarkness:dark_state` the server sends the client.
- The common config, `atouchofdarkness-common.toml`, sets the lit threshold, the Grace Period, the refill delay, and the Strike's damage and interval.
- The Torch, carried: `atouchofdarkness:torch` (stick and coal or charcoal) and `soul_torch` (over soul soil or soul sand) craft one, already burning, and never stack nor place. Held in either hand, and not by a Spectator, a Torch burns 5 min of the time of day (soul: 10 min), sleep and `/time` included, twice as fast in rain under the open sky, and lights the world at its carrier's eyes at 14 (soul: 10) through the block `atouchofdarkness:carried_light`, which follows them into air or water and removes itself once no carrier is there. In the inventory, a chest or on the ground it neither burns nor gives light. Burnt out or underwater it becomes `atouchofdarkness:spent_torch`, which relights in the hand with flint and steel, a fire charge or a Torch in the other, or at fire, lava or a lit campfire, for a quarter of the time, then is gone, underwater too. The data component `atouchofdarkness:burning` holds a Torch's burn and shows as its item bar, and as a countdown in its advanced tooltip (F3+H). The Torches' tooltips say they burn only while held, and the Spent Torch's how to relight it. A vanilla torch in a player's inventory becomes a Torch (soul to soul), and one broken in the world drops a Spent Torch. The new config `atouchofdarkness-light.toml` sets the Torch's burn time.
- The Campfire, placed: `atouchofdarkness:campfire` and `soul_campfire`, crafted in vanilla's campfire shape, are vanilla's campfire with fuel of their own, in vanilla's block entity, so they cook as vanilla's does and are in `minecraft:campfires`. Placed, one holds half its cap, half a day (soul: a day). Any furnace fuel used on it adds ten times its burn time up to the cap, the rest lost, and lights it again; a full one takes none. Lit, it burns by the time of day, sleep and `/time` included and its chunk unloaded too, twice as fast in rain under the open sky, the attachment `atouchofdarkness:fuel` holding its fuel and clock. Its light, the block state's `level`, is 15 / 12 / 9 / 6 above ¾ / ½ / ¼ / none of its cap (soul: 11 / 9 / 7 / 5), and it goes out dry. Broken, it drops vanilla's charcoal (soul: soul soil), never itself, and an axe breaks it as fast as vanilla's. The item doesn't burn. A vanilla campfire in a player's inventory becomes a Campfire (soul to soul). The light config's `campfire.cap` sets the cap.
- The Lamp, carried or placed: `atouchofdarkness:lamp` and `soul_lamp`, iron nuggets around a Torch (soul: a soul Torch), crafted with no fuel. Used in the air, or by an empty hand once placed, it turns off, or on if it has fuel. Any furnace fuel used on a placed one, or clicked onto one in an inventory as onto a bundle, adds ten times its burn time up to a day (soul: two), the rest lost; tools aren't fuel. Fuel never turns a placed Lamp on or off: one that's off stays off, and one on but dry shines again, while a carried one fuelled dry lights. On with fuel it gives 15 (soul: 10): held in either hand, as a Torch's carried light; placed, as vanilla's lantern, standing or hanging, which a pickaxe breaks as fast. It burns only then, by the time of day, rain or not, held or placed, its chunk unloaded too, and runs dry into a dark Lamp. Carried, the data component `atouchofdarkness:lamp_fuel` holds its fuel and shows as its item bar, and the marker `atouchofdarkness:lamp_on` that it is on with fuel, which its item model shows; placed, its block entity `atouchofdarkness:lamp` keeps both, and gives them back to the Lamp it drops. Any lantern in `minecraft:lanterns`, copper ones too, in a player's inventory becomes a full Lamp, on, and a soul lantern a soul Lamp. A Lamp that's on relights a Spent Torch in the other hand, as a Torch does. Its tooltip says to click fuel onto it and to right-click it placed to turn it on or off, and its advanced tooltip (F3+H) counts its fuel down. The light config's `lamp.cap` sets the cap.
- Redstone for the placed Lamp and Campfire: a placed Lamp switches as a vanilla door does, a rise in the signal turning it on and a fall off, and an empty hand toggling it in between until the next change; one switched on dry stays dark until fuelled, and one placed into a live signal is placed on, as a door placed so opens. Its block state's `on` holds whether it is switched on, `lit` whether it is on with fuel, and `powered` the signal it last saw. A comparator reads a placed Lamp's or a Campfire's fuel, 0 to 15 by the fifteenths of its cap it holds, rounded up, so 0 only once dry, and the reading falls as it burns. The Campfire doesn't switch by redstone.
- Vanilla's light goes out: the recipes of the torch, soul and copper torch, lantern, soul and copper lantern (waxed from honeycomb too), jack o'lantern, glowstone, sea lantern, end rod, redstone lamp, every copper bulb (waxed, and from honeycomb), all 17 candles, campfire and soul campfire are gone. A block in the new tag `atouchofdarkness:unlit` gives no light at all, for players, mobs and other mods: by default the redstone torch, redstone ore, enchanting table, ender chest, magma block, glow lichen, cave vines, sea pickle, amethyst clusters and buds, sculk catalyst, firefly bush, redstone lamp, copper bulbs, candles and candle cakes; a pack can add another mod's lamp. Redstone torches still power redstone, and every other light keeps vanilla's. The light config's `vanillaLight` switch brings the recipes back and empties the tag, whatever packs add, as the world's data loads or reloads; a block lit before, or in a chunk saved before the Pillar was added, keeps its light until a block beside it changes.
- The Nightbrand: a single Spent Torch in either hand of a survival or adventure player in the Dark (the main hand's alone if both hold one), on Peaceful too, darkens a tick for each tick they are there, its progress kept between dips in the data component `atouchofdarkness:darkening` and shown as its item bar and, once started, as a countdown in its advanced tooltip (F3+H), and after three Grace Periods becomes `atouchofdarkness:nightbrand`, which keeps forever and gives no light. A stack doesn't darken, nor one in a chest, on the ground, in a mob's hand or a fake player's, nor one held by a Lit, creative or spectator player. Each player darkens one a day, the day counted from dawn by the Overworld's clock, and death doesn't reset it. The light config's `nightbrand.gracePeriods` sets the Grace Periods it takes.
- The Lampad and her Votive: a Lamp of either kind standing on a gold block, kindled with a Torch of either kind by a player who may build there, becomes the Lampad, `atouchofdarkness:lampad`, and the gold her Votive, `atouchofdarkness:votive`; a Votive with no Lampad summons one the same way, and one that has a Lampad none. The Votive holds one slot each for fuel (any furnace fuel but a tool or one that leaves an item behind), Nightbrands and gold nuggets, each taking only its own, filled by hand through its screen or by a hopper or another mod's pipe from any side, and none taken out but by hand; broken, it drops itself and spills them, and its Lampad stands dormant for good. While her binding holds she carries one fuel item at a time from her Votive into the placed Lamps within 16 blocks of it either way across and 8 up or down, never Campfires nor carried Lamps, and only an item whose whole fits; each Lamp belongs to the nearest Votive with a Lampad in whose range it is. Her binding lasts 7 days of game time counted only while she is loaded, so she does nothing while her chunk is unloaded and catches nothing up. Once it fades she takes the next Nightbrand from her Votive, which renews it. Each binding takes a gold nugget from her Votive for each 8 Lamps in range, rounded up, at its start and again whenever more Lamps come into range than it has paid for; if the gold falls short she tends only the 8 a nugget paid for, nearest the Votive first. With no Nightbrand there she stands dormant as a statue until one comes; one is dormant from her summoning until her Votive has one. Taking gold from her Votive she makes a clink of gold and golden sparks over it, and taking fuel the allay's sound of an item taken; feeding a Lamp, the allay's chime of an item given, a crackle, and a burst of flame and smoke from the Lamp, the flame blue from a soul Lamp. She gives no light, and the Babau never strikes her. The light config's `lampad.binding` and `lampad.lampsPerNugget` set the binding and the Lamps a nugget pays for. Her spawn egg, `atouchofdarkness:lampad_spawn_egg`, makes one with no Votive, who stands dormant.
- Jade and EMI, both optional: with Jade, a placed Campfire's tooltip shows its fuel left as m:ss and the light it gives, or "Out" once dry, and a placed Lamp's "On" or "Off", then its fuel left or "Empty", sent from the server under the Jade plugin config `atouchofdarkness:fuel`. With EMI, six information pages: fuelling a placed light by hand and the caps, relighting a Spent Torch, vanilla's torches, lanterns and campfires becoming the Pillar's own, darkening a Spent Torch into a Nightbrand, summoning a Lampad to make the Votive, and vanilla's cheap light having no recipe until the `vanillaLight` switch. Neither is required nor nested in the jar.
- The Votive and the Lampad wear art from Malcolm Riley's unused-textures (CC BY 4.0, credited in NOTICE): the Votive's side and top are runed and framed aurichalcum plates in a gold block's colours, and the Lampad's robe and hair, and so her statue's, are woven fabrics. Her face, skin, trim and Nightbrand, her spawn egg and the Votive's screen are still generated.
